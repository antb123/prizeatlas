Award calendar 2027 (draft)

A draft calendar of when 16 major science and ideas prizes will name and present their winners in 2027. Most dates are projected: the 2026 date moved 52 weeks forward to the same weekday. Dashed dates are projections; solid dates are published by the prize. Where only the month is known, the event sits under that month as TBC.
