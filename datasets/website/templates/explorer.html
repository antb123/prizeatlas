    Career totals of early winners

    Laureates who won at least one award before turning 40, ranked by their lifetime points — these are finished careers, not people who are young now. Age comes from birth year, so it may be off by one.

    Awards by decade

    How many awards each prize family has handed out over time. The final decade is marked * because it is still running.

    Laureates by country

    Distinct laureates per modern country, top 25 shown. Every view counts a person once, however many awards they hold. Affiliation and citizenship count a laureate once under each listed country. The two rate views measure laureates against World Bank population, by birth country and by affiliation country; each needs at least 5 laureates and skips countries with no published figure.

    Age at first win, by subject

    How old laureates were when they won their first recorded award, grouped by subject. The bar spans the middle half of each subject — a quarter won younger, a quarter older — and the notch is the median. Mathematics stands apart because the Fields Medal is only awarded under 40. Organizations and laureates with no birth year are excluded.

    Where laureates go to win

    Each country's laureates per million people, counted twice: by where they were born, and by where they were working when the award came. A bar running right means the country hosts more laureates than it raises; running left means its laureates won elsewhere. Needs a published population figure and at least 5 laureates on one of the two measures.
